# Code of conduct

SeqQC contributors are expected to make participation respectful, inclusive,
and free from harassment. Be considerate, critique ideas rather than people,
respect privacy, and assume good faith while remaining evidence-driven.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, sexualized conduct,
threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained
disruption. Project maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect
the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method on the GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the
project elsewhere.

This concise policy may be replaced by a formally adopted community covenant
as the maintainer team grows.
